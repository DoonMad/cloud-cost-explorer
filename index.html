How this demo maps to the paper

Nodes: one candidate deployment for one pipeline stage. Directed edges: data flow from one stage to the next.

Edge/path cost: monthly resource costs plus data-transfer costs. The first resource is charged at the start; each later resource and its incoming transfer are charged once, avoiding double counting.

Paper method: the paper uses a Dijkstra variant with an O(V²) node scan. Comparison: DAG dynamic programming processes stages in order in O(V + E); both methods optimize the same finite graph and additive score.

Model boundary: this demo is a linear pipeline. It connects every feasible candidate in one stage to every candidate in the next; it does not model a required multi-branch architecture as a whole.

Extension: the paper evaluates Google Cloud pricing scenarios. Multiple providers here demonstrate an extension; prices and compatibility are user-supplied assumptions. The Paper ETL example follows the paper's three-stage regional flow, but its prices are illustrative.
